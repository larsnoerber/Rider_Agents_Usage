Providers & page bar

Select once: checked providers refresh in the background and appear in the bar after website access is granted. Unchecked providers are hidden and their reads stop.

Drag the bar using its move handle. The position is remembered across websites. Changing the default position clears your custom position.

Website access

Grant access to display the bar on ordinary websites and read selected providers when their tabs are closed. Sign in on each provider's website first.

Open-tab URLs are checked locally to locate provider tabs. Browsing history is not saved or exported.

For access to individual providers, use the buttons above. Manage all-websites permissions in the browser's extension settings. Browser settings pages and extension stores cannot display the bar.

Appearance & quota warnings

Percentages turn amber at or below this threshold and red at 10% remaining or below. A threshold below 10% also lowers the red threshold. GitHub displays consumed quota.

Hover a provider for its last update. Open quota details for reset countdowns. Pin AgentMeter in the browser's Extensions menu to see its badge beside the address bar.

Saved usage & missing values

Only numeric quota values and their observation/reset times are cached. Saved values are labelled and do not prove a current login. Missing values stay as dashes; hover or open details for the reason.

Claude documents usage bars for paid subscription plans. A Free account may not report quota percentages; missing data alone does not identify your plan.

Credentials, account identifiers and conversations are never stored. Provider sessions are used only for requests to that provider. The bar reads no content on other websites.
